# Creative Brief — Platzidle Launch Campaign

**Reto 09 · Vibe Coders League (Edición 2.0)**

## Product

**Platzidle** — a Wordle clone where the secret word is always a tech term, and when you solve it, one tap takes you to the **real Platzi course** to actually learn it.
Live: https://platzidle.robertzu43.workers.dev

## Target audience

Aspiring developers and self-taught tech learners in the Platzi community — plus daily word-game (Wordle) fans who want their morning ritual to make them a little better at tech.

- Curious, motivated, early in their learning journey.
- Already have a daily word-game habit, or love the idea of one.
- Want *real* progress, not just entertainment.

## Main promise

**"Guess the word. Actually learn the tech."**
Every day a new tech term — and one click away, the Platzi course to master it.

## The differentiator (campaign spine)

It's not just a game. **Solve the term → tap the link → you're in the real Platzi course.**
The game is the hook; real learning is the destination. Every piece lands on this payoff.

## Tone & personality

Motivational, close, nerd-friendly. Platzi energy: green, community, "you can do this." Never corporate. Speaks like a peer who's rooting for you.

## Insight

Millions play their Wordle every morning out of pure habit. What if that 2-minute ritual made you a slightly better developer every day?

## Tagline

**"Learn tech by playing — one term a day, one course a click away."**

## Message hierarchy (what every asset must convey, in order)

1. It's a Wordle you can actually play (instantly recognizable green tiles).
2. The words are **tech terms**.
3. **Solve → real Platzi course.** (the payoff)
4. It's free, daily, shareable — play now.

## Do / Don't

- **Do** use real terms and real Platzi course names (verified in reto-08).
- **Do** keep the green-tile motif in every piece.
- **Don't** promise courses that don't exist, or over-design away the simplicity.
- **Don't** make it look like generic AI art — it must read as *this* product.
